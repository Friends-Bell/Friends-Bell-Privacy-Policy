# Delete Your Friends Bell Account

**Last updated:** YYYY-MM-DD <!-- Set to the publish date. -->

This page explains how to delete your **Friends Bell** account and the data linked to it. Friends Bell is built by **Blink22**.

The account is optional. You create one only to send and receive nudges, by signing in with your phone number. If you never signed in, you have no account to delete. Everything else the App keeps is on your device (see [Section 4](#4-what-stays-on-your-phone)).

---

## 1. Delete Your Account in the App

1. Open Friends Bell.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account**, then confirm.

Your account is deleted right away, and the App signs you out. This can't be undone. If you sign up again later with the same number, you start with a new, empty account.

---

## 3. What Is Deleted

| Data | |
|---|---|
| Your account: your phone number and sign-in record | ✅ Deleted |
| Your profile: display name, avatar color, who can nudge you, and the App's language | ✅ Deleted |
| Nudges you sent and received, including the copies your friends see | ✅ Deleted |
| The people you blocked | ✅ Deleted |
| Your devices' notification tokens | ✅ Deleted |
| Counts of your recent requests, kept to prevent abuse | ✅ Deleted |
| The protected (hashed) copy of your number that lets friends find you | ✅ Deleted |

Once your account is deleted, friends can no longer find you or nudge you, and the nudges between you disappear from their app too.

---

## 4. What Stays on Your Phone

Your contacts, groups, reminders, quick reminders, notes, history and preferences were never uploaded, so deleting your account doesn't touch them. You can keep using Friends Bell without an account.

To remove them as well, uninstall the App or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Friends Bell → Storage → Clear storage).

**Friends Bell Pro** belongs to your Google Play account, not your Friends Bell account. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription; manage it in Google Play.

---

## 5. Timeline

* **In the App:** immediately.
* **By email:** within 30 days of your request.
* **System logs** at our service provider (Google Firebase), which may record your account ID, are deleted automatically within 30 days.
* **WhatsApp sign-in records** (a sign-in link record and fraud-prevention counters, if you signed in with WhatsApp) aren't part of your account. They're deleted automatically, usually within 3 days of the last sign-in link request. A sign-in message stays in your WhatsApp chat until you delete it there.
* **Analytics and crash data** don't carry your account ID, phone number or name. They're deleted automatically within 90 days, as described in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

Even without deleting your account, every nudge is deleted automatically after 30 days.
